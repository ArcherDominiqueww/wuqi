I'm a fourth-year PhD student in Linguistics at University of Connecticut. I'm a theoretical linguist working on Syntax, Semantics, Morphology, and the way they interact, aiming at understanding the human language faculty through the lens of language structures and meaning. I also hold interests in the social and sociological meaning of languages.

Before diving into language structure, my background was about analyzing how narratives are structured by language.
